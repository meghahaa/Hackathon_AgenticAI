# Risk Register: Discharge-Planning & Follow-up Copilot

Scale: Likelihood / Impact = Low · Medium · High. *Inherent* = before controls, *Residual* = after.
Categories: OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025 (LLM01–LLM10) and NIST AI RMF 1.0 function +
NIST AI 600-1 (GenAI profile) risk. Every mitigation cites the committed control **and** the
evidence that it works (a test or a machine-generated log/trace record).
